# Sistema 90G — International Brand Platform V1

Status: canonical brand decision
Date: 2026-09-14

## 1. Strategic decision

Sistema 90G remains explicitly Italian as it expands internationally.

The origin is not hidden or neutralised. It is deliberately used as a
point of differentiation.

The current primary domain remains:

`https://sistema90g.it/`

The `.it` domain is therefore part of the brand identity, not merely a
temporary technical constraint.

## 2. International positioning

Sistema 90G helps private customers identify problems, inconsistencies
and risky kitchen decisions before they become costly or difficult to
correct.

It does not sell kitchens.

It does not represent manufacturers or retailers.

It must not position itself primarily as a generic interior-design
studio.

The differentiating value is independent verification, prevention and
better decision-making.

## 3. Brand idea

**Italian method. Independent checks. Better kitchen decisions.**

Supporting promise:

**Spot problems before they become expensive mistakes.**

Preferred positioning expression:

**Sistema 90G — An Italian method for better kitchen decisions.**

## 4. What “Italian method” means

Italian identity must not be reduced to style, luxury or aesthetics.

Within Sistema 90G it represents:

- a strong culture of kitchens and everyday living;
- attention to function as well as appearance;
- attention to detail;
- understanding the relationship between space, product and use;
- attention to the practical consequences of decisions;
- the principle of doing things carefully and well;
- identifying a problem while it can still be corrected.

## 5. What it does not mean

International communication must not turn Sistema 90G into:

- generic “Italian kitchen design”;
- a style or luxury brand;
- a kitchen retailer;
- a manufacturer representative;
- an inspiration-only interiors website.

`Made in Italy` is not the default service claim.

Preferred language includes:

- `Italian method`
- `Italian expertise`
- `Italian approach`
- `Developed in Italy`
- `Born in Italy`

## 6. Customer-first invariant

Italian identity never replaces the customer's problem.

The canonical communication sequence remains:

customer -> problem -> understanding -> useful next step -> service only
when useful.

The public website must therefore continue to lead with the real
question, risk or decision faced by the customer.

## 7. Independence

Sistema 90G must continue to make clear that it:

- does not sell the kitchen being reviewed;
- does not receive commission on the sale;
- does not represent third-party brands;
- can conclude that no paid service is needed;
- separates verified information from assumptions and missing data.

## 8. International localisation

Italian origin is constant.

Localisation is market-specific.

Each market may require different:

- terminology;
- units;
- currency;
- examples;
- SEO queries;
- consumer information;
- contractual rules;
- tax and invoicing presentation;
- customer expectations.

A localised market must never be a mechanical translation of another
market.

## 9. UK application

The first international implementation is `en-GB`.

The UK website remains under:

`https://sistema90g.it/en/`

UK English terminology and metric measurements remain mandatory.

The Italian identity should be most visible on:

- the English homepage;
- the Method page;
- the About page;
- institutional brand descriptions.

Service and problem pages should continue to lead primarily with the
customer's situation rather than repeating the Italian-origin message.

## 10. Future markets

The same brand platform applies to future markets including the United
States, Canada, Australia and New Zealand.

The Italian identity remains constant while each market receives its own
localisation and commercial/legal review.

## 11. Guardrail

The success criterion is not that every page says “Italian”.

The success criterion is that a customer can understand that Sistema 90G
is an independent Italian method, available in their market, whose
purpose is to identify problems before they become expensive mistakes.
