Why I look at kitchens this way

The same kitchen changes depending on when you look at it.

During design, you need to understand whether the solution can work. During the sale, you need to understand what is actually being proposed.

During installation, measurements, site conditions, tolerances and compatibility become concrete. After delivery comes the most practical test: everyday use.

Seeing these stages as one connected process changes how a design is read before it is ordered.
01

Design

Layout, measurements, openings, ergonomics and relationship with the room.

02

Sales

Proposal, materials, appliances, services, inclusions and exclusions.

03

Installation

The real room, site conditions, dimensions and installation constraints.

04

After-sales

What happens when the kitchen is finally used.

Independence

I do not sell the kitchen I am reviewing.

Sistema 90G does not sell furniture, does not represent brands and does not receive commission on the sale.

That does not make the retailer unnecessary. The final measured survey, feasibility within its own modular system, the order, installation and after-sales support remain essential responsibilities of the business supplying the kitchen.

My role is to reach that conversation with more precise questions and, where possible, problems already identified.

What this means in practice

Three rules that matter more than a presentation.

01

Look beyond the drawing.

A kitchen is not assessed only by how it appears on plan. Openings, circulation, appliances, people and movement have to work together.

02

Separate what is known from what still needs verification.

If a measurement, product data sheet or confirmation from the retailer, manufacturer, installer or another competent professional is missing, that is stated.

03

Technology supports the work. It does not decide for me.

Digital tools and AI can help organise, compare and check information. Final review and assessment remain human.

Clear limits

Knowing where to stop is part of the work.

Sistema 90G does not replace specialist building, structural, services or other technical checks that require an appropriately qualified or competent professional.

It does not certify the retailer's final measured survey and does not turn an independent design basis into a production order.

When something needs confirmation from a retailer, manufacturer, installer or competent professional, the useful result is to identify it and state it clearly.
