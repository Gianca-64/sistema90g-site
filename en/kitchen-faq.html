Real questions about kitchens

Designing, choosing and ordering a kitchen: questions worth asking before you decide.

These are not generic FAQs. They are questions that can change a decision, from the first layout to comparing proposals and signing the order.

1 · Plan

Can I plan the kitchen before choosing a retailer?

Yes. An independent design direction can clarify layout, priorities, functions and constraints before you enter a showroom. The chosen retailer can then adapt it to the final measured survey, its modular system and the products of the selected brand.

2 · Assess

How can I tell whether a kitchen design is good?

Assess the design during real use. Circulation, openings, appliances, storage, worktops, people moving and the constraints of the room need to work together.

3 · Compare

How do I compare two kitchen quotes?

Do not compare only the total. Make the proposals comparable by checking the composition, materials, appliances, accessories, fabrication, installation, included services, exclusions and conditions.

90G Quote & Order Review → Quote and value case →

4 · Check

What should I check before signing a kitchen order?

Before signing, the design, measurements, openings, utility positions, materials, appliance models, fabrication, installation, included services, exclusions and responsibilities should be clear.

5 · Coordinate

Should kitchen utility positions be decided before or after the design?

Final utility positions should follow a sufficiently defined kitchen design. Deciding them too early can unnecessarily constrain the composition or require later changes. Final technical verification remains specific to the site and installation.

6 · Choose finishes

Is a matt or gloss kitchen better?

There is no universally better choice. Light, colour, door size, flooring, nearby materials, fingerprints and maintenance all affect the appearance and practicality of matt and gloss finishes.

7 · Review

When is an independent kitchen review useful?

It is useful when you already have a design or quote and still have questions about layout, measurements, circulation, openings, ergonomics, appliances, materials, price or conditions before ordering.
