Your situation

You want to understand what you are actually buying.

This service is for when you have one or more quotes, a commercial proposal or order documentation and want to understand them more clearly before making a decision.

Composition and products

We read what is described: units, materials, hardware, accessories, appliances, product codes and specifications where available.

Included and excluded services

We make transport, installation, surveys, fabrication, connections, alterations, disposal and other cost-relevant items easier to identify.

Differences between documents

Where several offers or an order confirmation exist, we highlight differences, changes, unclear items and points to verify before proceeding.

The lowest price is not the point

Two quotes are comparable only when they genuinely describe the same supply.

A total or discount percentage alone does not show what you are buying. The review makes products, equipment, work, exclusions and responsibilities easier to compare.

What is reviewed

  • the kitchen composition described in the documents;
  • declared materials and finishes;
  • hardware, accessories and components;
  • appliances, models and codes where stated;
  • included work and fabrication;
  • delivery, installation and other services;
  • excluded or unclear items;
  • differences between multiple quotes;
  • differences between quote and order confirmation;
  • points requiring clarification before signing or ordering.

What you receive

An organised review showing what is clear, what changes between proposals, what is missing, possible practical or economic consequences and the questions to take back to the retailer.

What is not included

The review does not determine whether a price is objectively correct or provide a formal valuation of a product's commercial value. It also does not include a new kitchen design.

What about the kitchen design?

If the main question concerns layout, measurements, clearances, openings, ergonomics, clashes or compatibility with the room, the appropriate service may instead be the 90G Kitchen Review.

What information may be needed

Useful documents can include quotes, order confirmations, kitchen plans, unit schedules, product sheets, appliance lists, sales conditions and other documents connected with the supply.

You do not need to know what is missing in advance: the free initial assessment also checks whether the documentation is sufficient.

Delivery time

Indicative delivery within 2 working days after complete material has been received and payment has been made.

Price

€129. The price is fixed for the standard scope of 90G Quote & Order Review.
