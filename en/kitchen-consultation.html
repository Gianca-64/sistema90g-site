Your situation

You need to choose and want to understand which alternative suits your case better.

The 90G Kitchen Consultation is for a specific question that does not require a review of the whole kitchen design or the development of a new solution.

Brands and products

Compare brands, ranges or products when the choice depends on characteristics, real use, maintenance, perceived quality and practical constraints.

Appliances

Compare models, features and alternatives when the decision does not require the overall kitchen layout to be redesigned.

Materials and finishes

Worktops, fronts, colours, splashbacks, flooring and other defined material or finish choices.

Components and accessories

Hardware, internal fittings, sinks, taps, lighting and other components that can be evaluated separately.

Specific alternatives

Two already identified options when you want to understand practical benefits, limits and consequences without redesigning the kitchen.

Other defined decisions

Other questions can fit the consultation when they are sufficiently limited and can be assessed from the available material.

The boundary of the service

A consultation must not become a hidden full review or design project.

If the case shows that a serious answer requires checking the whole composition, analysing several linked documents or developing a new solution, we tell you before the service begins.

What is assessed

We assess the decision against the available information: requirements, expected use, technical characteristics, compatibility, maintenance, advantages, limitations and consequences relevant to your case.

What you receive

A reasoned answer comparing the alternatives, explaining the criteria used and identifying anything that cannot be verified with sufficient certainty.

What is not included

It does not include a full kitchen design review, extensive analysis of quotes or orders, a new kitchen design, design alternatives, measured surveys or work reserved for qualified professionals.

What information may be needed

Depending on the decision, a written description, photographs, product links or technical data sheets may be enough. In other cases we may need part of the kitchen design, measurements or technical documentation.

The free initial assessment also establishes whether enough information is available for a serious answer.

Delivery time

Indicative delivery within 1 working day after complete material has been received and payment has been made.

Price

€79. The price is fixed for the standard scope of 90G Kitchen Consultation.

Before you proceed, we check free of charge whether your case fits the standard scope.
