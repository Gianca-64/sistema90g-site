Your situation

You already have a kitchen design and want to know whether there are critical issues.

The 90G Kitchen Review starts from an existing solution. It does not redesign the kitchen; it identifies what deserves attention before the order or other hard-to-reverse steps.

Space and layout

We read the kitchen organisation, clearances, openings, ergonomics, working zones and relationship with the room.

Clashes and compatibility

Where the information allows, we check possible clashes between units, doors, drawers, appliances, walls, room doors and other elements.

Measurements, services and feasibility

We check the consistency of available information and flag anything that cannot be confirmed without surveys, technical data or further checks.

The objective

Identify today what could become a problem tomorrow.

An independent review does not have to find an error. It must distinguish what appears consistent, what presents a critical issue and what cannot be verified with the information available.

What is checked

  • layout and functional organisation;
  • clearances and spaces needed to use the kitchen;
  • opening of fronts, drawers and appliances;
  • ergonomics and simultaneous use;
  • possible clashes with walls, doors, windows or other constraints;
  • appliances and their integration into the composition;
  • available measurements and information;
  • utility points where readable from the documentation;
  • compatibility between room and kitchen composition;
  • items requiring further verification.

What you receive

An organised review separating identified critical issues, possible consequences, consistent elements, missing information and points that still need verification.

What is not included

The 90G Kitchen Review does not include development of a new solution, design alternatives, an executive survey, services engineering, calculations or work reserved for qualified professionals.

If the problem genuinely requires redesign, that is identified before the service begins.

What about the quote?

If the main problem is understanding what an offer includes, comparing quotes, reading product codes or checking differences between quote and order, use the 90G Quote & Order Review.

What information may be needed

Depending on the case, useful material can include a floor plan, kitchen plan, elevations, renders, measurements, photographs, appliance data and information about utility points.

The free initial assessment also checks whether the available material is sufficient.

Delivery time

Indicative delivery within 2 working days after complete material has been received and payment has been made.

Price

€149. The price is fixed for the standard scope of 90G Kitchen Review.
