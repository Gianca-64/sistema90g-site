Cookie Policy

What the website stores, when analytics may start and how you can change your choice.

Last updated: 13 September 2026

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains the storage and access technologies used on the English-language Sistema 90G website.

Some preferences may be stored in your browser's local storage rather than in a traditional browser cookie.

2. Strictly necessary storage

Sistema 90G may store the choice you make in the cookie banner so that the website can remember whether analytics has been accepted or rejected.

This preference is necessary to respect your privacy choice and does not require analytics consent.

3. Analytics

Google Analytics is used only after you choose to accept analytics.

If you reject analytics, Sistema 90G does not intentionally activate Google Analytics through its consent runtime.

Analytics is used to understand how the website is used and to improve navigation, content and customer journeys.

4. Your choice

You can accept or reject analytics. Rejecting analytics does not prevent you from using the main functions of the website.

You can change your choice later by selecting Cookie settings in the footer.

5. Third-party services

When a third-party service is used, that provider may apply its own storage or access technologies in accordance with its own policies and the consent or legal basis applicable to that service.

Payment through SumUp takes place in the separate payment flow. SumUp is responsible for the technologies used on its own payment environment.
