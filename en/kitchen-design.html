Your situation

You need to develop a solution for your kitchen.

The 90G Kitchen Design is for when the solution still needs to be defined, or when what already exists is not sufficient to reach a coherent configuration for the real space, requirements and constraints.

Organise the space

Layout, functions, storage, working zones, clearances, openings and the relationship between kitchen and room are considered together.

Anticipate critical issues

Possible clashes, incompatibilities, constraints and conditions that may create problems during use, ordering or installation are considered while the solution is developed.

Make the solution understandable

The design includes the drawings needed to understand the solution, main choices and points that still require confirmation in later stages.

The method

Designing also means looking for problems before they appear.

A solution can look correct on plan and reveal problems when doors or appliances open, several areas are used at once, real measurements change or utility positions are finalised.

What is considered

  • requirements and patterns of use;
  • overall organisation of the space;
  • kitchen layout and functions;
  • storage and working surfaces;
  • ergonomics and use sequences;
  • clearances and space needed to use the kitchen;
  • doors, windows and other openings;
  • appliances and their integration;
  • available utility points;
  • possible clashes between elements;
  • feasibility based on available data;
  • predictable issues in later stages;
  • items requiring final confirmation or checking.

What you receive

One main kitchen solution with drawings useful for understanding layout, functions and organisation, together with an explanation of the main choices and any critical issues or points still to verify.

Drawings and visualisations

The type and number of drawings depend on the complexity of the case and are defined before the service starts. They may include a floor plan, elevations, sections, three-dimensional views and other representations needed to make the solution understandable.

What is not included

It does not include an executive measured survey, structural or services engineering, statutory applications, calculations or work reserved for qualified professionals. It also does not replace the final checks required before ordering and installation.

Working with your retailer

Sistema 90G develops the design independently, without being tied to a particular brand or catalogue. Your chosen retailer subsequently verifies the measured survey, available modules, products and codes, final feasibility, quotation and order.

What information may be needed

Useful material can include a floor plan, measurements, photographs, utility positions, appliance information and details about how you need the kitchen to work.

The free initial assessment also establishes whether the available material is sufficient or what needs to be obtained before starting.

Delivery time

Indicative delivery within 3 working days after complete material has been received and payment has been made.
