Your situation

The kitchen has arrived and something is not working as it should.

The problem may involve installation, components, measurements, materials, appliances, clashes or differences from what was designed or ordered.

Reconstruct what happened

We compare photographs, design documents, quote, order, technical data and available correspondence to reconstruct the sequence and context.

Separate facts from hypotheses

We distinguish verifiable evidence from possible causes and from anything that cannot be determined from the available material.

Identify what to check next

Where the issue cannot be resolved from documents alone, we indicate what measurements, checks or additional expertise may be needed.

The method

Before attributing a cause, separate what is verified from what is only possible.

A photograph may show an anomaly without proving its cause. A design may describe an intended solution without proving how it was actually installed. The analysis keeps those levels separate.

What types of problem can be analysed

  • possible installation error;
  • incorrect or missing component;
  • dimensional or alignment problems;
  • visible defects or anomalies;
  • worktop or fabrication problems;
  • fronts, drawers, hardware or accessories;
  • materials and finishes;
  • appliances and their integration;
  • clashes during use;
  • differences from the design or order;
  • missing items or an incomplete kitchen;
  • other documentable issues after delivery or installation.

What you receive

Where applicable, the analysis separates:

  • VERIFIED: what is supported by the available evidence;
  • POSSIBLE CAUSES: explanations compatible with the evidence, without presenting them as established facts;
  • NOT VERIFIABLE: what cannot be established from the available material;
  • FURTHER CHECKS: what should be measured, inspected or investigated further.

What information may be needed

Useful material can include detailed photographs, video, kitchen design, floor plan, quote, order confirmation, product data sheets, appliance information, delivery notes, correspondence with the retailer or installer and a chronological description of what happened.

You do not need to have everything: the free initial assessment also identifies which information is essential.

What is not included

The 90G Kitchen Problem Analysis is not a legal expert report, legal advice, a statutory technical certification or a formally certified report.

If the case requires work reserved for a qualified professional, a formal expert report or legal assistance, we identify the need to involve the appropriate person.

Delivery time
