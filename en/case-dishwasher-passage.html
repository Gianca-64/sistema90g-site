Practical kitchen case

The kitchen looks comfortable. Then you open the dishwasher and the passage disappears.

This issue is easy to miss in a render: the composition fits the room, but an open appliance can turn a circulation route into a point of conflict during real use.

Analysis by Gian Carlo Primo · Updated 8 July 2026

Quick answer

The distance between the units is not enough.

You need to understand how much usable passage remains with the dishwasher fully open and whether that point connects the fridge, an exit, the dining area or another frequently used route. If two people use the route at the same time, the conflict can become a daily problem.

The real-life situation

One person is loading the dishwasher. Another needs to reach the fridge or move out of the kitchen.

With the door closed, the passage may look sufficient. When the dishwasher is open, it occupies part of that circulation space.

Why the render is not enough

A render shows the kitchen closed. The kitchen is used with doors, drawers and appliances open.

Real opening

What matters is the space that remains when the dishwasher door is fully open.

Route

Check who needs to pass through that exact point and which functions the route connects.

Simultaneous use

The conflict appears when one person loads the dishwasher while another person is using or crossing the kitchen.

Limit of this public analysis: without final room measurements, the actual depth of the appliance and the precise position of the cabinet fronts, it is not possible to state a numerically safe passage.

The consequence

The risk is discovering the problem after installation.

If the route is blocked repeatedly, the inconvenience is not occasional. Changing the layout, a unit or the appliance position after ordering can become more difficult and expensive.
