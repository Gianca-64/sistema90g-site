Before you order

9 questions I would ask about your kitchen.

This is not a test to pass. These are simple questions that often reveal what a render does not show.

If you want to look more widely before deciding, see the questions worth asking before choosing and buying a kitchen .

  1. Does the dishwasher leave enough room to pass when it is open?
  2. Can the fridge door open fully?
  3. Do occupied stools reduce the clearance around the island?
  4. Can doors, drawers and appliances open at the same time?
  5. Does the quote clearly identify materials and appliances?
  6. Do you know exactly what is excluded from the price?
  7. Do the utility positions match the current version of the design?
  8. Were the measurements checked again after later changes?
  9. Do you know which points still need checking before you order?

Did you answer “I don't know” more than once?
That is exactly the kind of uncertainty worth resolving before installation, not afterwards.
