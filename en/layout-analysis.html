Does the layout work for everyday life?

We review a layout already represented. We distinguish consistent elements, issues and information that cannot yet be verified.

What we analyse

  • Room organisation and relationships between functions.
  • Circulation, entrances, connections and access.
  • Furniture footprints and the space needed to use them.
  • Door, window and furniture openings and possible clashes.
  • How the layout relates to the needs you describe.
  • Constraints visible in the documents, missing information and further checks needed.

Conclusions depend on the material available. We explicitly identify any measurement or constraint that cannot be verified.

What you receive

An annotated floor plan of the layout reviewed, with a concise report covering strengths, issues, practical consequences and recommendations for improvement.

Annotations explain where a problem arises and what needs reconsideration. They do not constitute a new layout design or construction drawing.

One final clarification of the findings is included, without developing new layouts or design alternatives.

One service, whatever the rooms

The analysis may cover one room or several rooms in the same home: kitchen, living room, entrance, bedrooms or utility spaces. The fixed total price is €199 EUR, regardless of the number or type of rooms.

We start from an existing layout or a proposal already shown on a plan, including the functions and furniture to be reviewed. A professional drawing is not required, provided the material makes the layout understandable.

What is not included

The service does not include a new design, new furnished layouts, design alternatives, renders, product selection, surveys, structural or building services design, planning applications, certifications or work reserved for qualified professionals.

A blank floor plan with a request to decide where all furniture should go requires design work and falls outside this analysis.

What information is needed

Send your floor plan with available measurements, the layout you want reviewed, photographs and your requirements. Identify furniture to keep and known constraints such as openings, radiators and utility points.

You may start with a scan or a photograph of the plan. The free initial assessment identifies what is readable and what information is still needed. Missing measurements are not invented.

Price and getting started

€199 EUR in total. No surcharge based on the number or type of rooms. VAT is not applied under the Italian flat-rate tax regime; any Italian stamp duty is covered by Sistema 90G.

The free initial assessment clarifies your concern and checks whether the material supports a useful analysis. If recovering a measurement or asking for clarification is enough, we tell you.

The analysis content and delivery time are confirmed before you proceed. Work begins after the required material and payment have been received.

How this differs from kitchen services

Layout Analysis reviews room organisation and the relationships between spaces. The 90G Kitchen Review examines the specific details of an existing kitchen design, including the composition, appliances and technical compatibility where verifiable.

The 90G Kitchen Design develops a kitchen solution. That is a different service from reviewing a layout already proposed.
