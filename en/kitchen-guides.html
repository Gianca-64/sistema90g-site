Common questions before the design

Where do you start when planning a kitchen?

Start with the room and the way it will actually be used: openings, circulation, people, storage, appliances and everyday habits. The composition comes afterwards, because it has to respond to those conditions.

Can I plan the kitchen before choosing a retailer?

Yes. An independent design direction can be developed first. The chosen retailer then verifies and adapts it to the final measured survey, its modular system and the products of its brand.

How can I tell whether a kitchen layout is functional?

Simulate what happens during real use: doors and appliances open, people moving, seats occupied and work areas active at the same time.

Should the kitchen layout or the utility positions be defined first?

Where possible, define at least the overall kitchen layout first so utility positions can be coordinated with the actual elements. Final technical verification remains specific to the site and installation.
