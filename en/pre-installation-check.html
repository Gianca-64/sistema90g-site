Your situation

The kitchen has already been ordered but has not yet been installed.

At this stage you are no longer choosing the kitchen. We compare the final design, order, room and available utility information before the installers arrive.

Final documentation

Where available, we compare the final design, order confirmation, later changes and information about the items that will be installed.

Room and utility points

We read available measurements, walls, openings, flooring, finishes and utility positions to identify visible incompatibilities.

Clashes before installation

We look for situations that might otherwise emerge only when units, worktops and appliances are physically installed.

The last preventive check

It is better to discover a critical issue before the units arrive.

An issue found before installation may still leave time for a check, amendment or clarification. The same issue discovered during installation can lead to more complex work, delay or additional cost.

What is checked

  • available final kitchen design;
  • order confirmation and linked documentation;
  • changes made after the design;
  • available room measurements;
  • doors, windows and other room constraints;
  • documented utility positions;
  • water, waste, sockets and power supplies where documented;
  • planned appliances and their positions;
  • possible clashes between kitchen and room;
  • delivery and installation conditions where verifiable;
  • differences or inconsistencies between documents;
  • points requiring confirmation before installation.

What you receive

An organised review of identified critical issues, consistent elements, missing information and points to check with the retailer, installer or appropriate qualified professional before installation.

What is not included

This check does not replace the final measured survey, the retailer's or installer's execution responsibility, specialist utility checks, site supervision or work reserved for qualified professionals.

This is not a new design

The service starts from a kitchen that has already been ordered. If a problem requiring a design change emerges, we identify it, but development of a new solution is not automatically included in the Pre-installation Check.

What information may be needed

Useful material can include the final design, order confirmation, floor plan, measured survey, up-to-date room photographs, measurements, utility drawings, appliance specifications and correspondence about changes made after ordering.

The free initial assessment establishes which documents are actually needed for your case.

Delivery time

Indicative delivery within 2 working days after complete material has been received and payment has been made.

Price

€179. The price is fixed for the standard scope of 90G Pre-installation Check.
