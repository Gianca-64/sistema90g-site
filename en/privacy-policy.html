2. What information may be processed?

Depending on how you use the website, Free Entry and the customer Portal, Sistema 90G may process:

  • your name, surname, email address, telephone number and country;
  • the description of your kitchen, your questions and the information you choose to provide;
  • floor plans, quotations, designs, photographs, measurements, product information and other files you choose to upload;
  • service, order, billing and payment-status information;
  • messages and other communications connected with your case;
  • technical and security information generated when you use the website and Portal;
  • analytics information where you have consented to analytics technologies.

Please do not include personal information that is not relevant to the kitchen case.

3. Why we use the information and our lawful bases

Personal data is used only where there is an appropriate lawful basis.

  • Free Entry and pre-contract steps: to answer your request and assess what service, if any, may be useful. The lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • Providing a paid service: to manage the case, receive material, take payment, perform the agreed service and deliver the result. The lawful basis is performance of the contract.
  • Accounting, tax and legal records: where processing is required to comply with a legal obligation.
  • Security, abuse prevention, service integrity and defence of legal claims: where necessary for legitimate interests, balanced against your rights and interests.
  • Analytics technologies: where consent is required, analytics is enabled only after your consent. You can withdraw that choice through Cookie settings.

4. Who may receive personal data?

Data may be made available only as necessary to service providers that support the operation of Sistema 90G, including hosting and infrastructure providers, email delivery providers, payment provider SumUp and analytics provider Google where analytics consent has been given.

Information may also be disclosed to professional advisers or public authorities where this is necessary or required by law.

Sistema 90G does not sell your personal data to advertisers.

5. Payments

Payments are processed through SumUp. Sistema 90G does not need to receive your complete payment-card credentials.

Sistema 90G records only the information required to identify the payment, its status and the related service.

6. International transfers

Sistema 90G is established in Italy. Personal data relating to UK customers may therefore be processed in Italy.

The European Economic Area, including Italy, is currently covered by UK adequacy regulations for international transfers of personal data.

Some service providers may process information in other countries. Where UK data protection law treats this as a restricted transfer, Sistema 90G will use an applicable lawful transfer mechanism, such as adequacy or appropriate safeguards.

7. How long information is kept

Personal data is not kept indefinitely merely because it has been collected.

Retention is determined by the purpose for which the information is held, the need to provide and document the service, to deal with queries or complaints, to maintain security and audit records, and any applicable legal, accounting or tax retention obligations.

When information is no longer required for those purposes, it is deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

8. Your rights

Subject to the conditions and exceptions in applicable data protection law, you may have rights including access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent without affecting processing already carried out lawfully before withdrawal.

To exercise a data protection right, contact info@sistema90g.it .

9. Complaints

If you have concerns about how Sistema 90G handles personal data, please contact us first at info@sistema90g.it .

If UK data protection law applies, you also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

ICO information is available at ico.org.uk .

10. UK representative — pre-launch blocker

Sistema 90G is established outside the UK and is preparing a dedicated UK service.

Before the UK service is released, Sistema 90G will either appoint the UK representative required by Article 27 UK GDPR and publish the representative's contact details here, or retain documented professional advice confirming that the statutory exemption applies.

This privacy notice is therefore a pre-launch draft and must not be treated as the final UK privacy notice until that point has been resolved.

11. Human review and artificial intelligence

Digital tools and artificial intelligence may support the organisation and analysis of information.

They do not autonomously make the final technical decision supplied to the customer. Final review and assessment remain human.
